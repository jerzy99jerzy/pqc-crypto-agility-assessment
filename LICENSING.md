## Which one applies to you

**AGPL-3.0 (default, no fee).** You may run, study, modify and redistribute this
software, including offering it as a network service, **provided that** you make
the complete corresponding source of your version - including any
modifications - available to every user who interacts with it over a network,
under AGPL-3.0. The network-use clause (AGPL section 13) is the point: unlike
GPL, running a modified version as a hosted service (SaaS) triggers the
obligation to release your source. If you can meet that obligation, you owe
nothing and need no other permission.

## Why this model

The assessment methodology, the collection pipeline, and the detection content
are the value. AGPL keeps that value open and forces anyone who turns it into a
competing service to contribute their changes back, while the commercial option
funds continued development for organisations that cannot accept copyleft. This
is the same open-core / dual-license structure used by projects such as GitLab
and Grafana.
